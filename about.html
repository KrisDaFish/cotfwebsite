about us

Who are we?

We are a group of dedicated high school students, from all around the world, committed to making Python simple, entertaining and accessible to everyone. Our mission is to inspire future generations by sharing our passion for coding and technology. Through engaging lessons and community involvement, we aim to spark creativity, construct confidence, and help students develop a strong basis in Python to prepare them for a successful future.
